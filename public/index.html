FAQ

QUESTIONS, answered.

Anything else? Ask us in the chat or email hello@plumb.studio.

How much does a project cost?

We quote each project after a short conversation, once we know the pages, features or workflows involved. You get a fixed price in writing before any work begins, and it doesn't change unless the scope does.

How quickly do you reply in the chat?

When the team is online (the status at the top of the chat shows this live), usually within a few minutes. When we're away, leave your email in the chat and we'll reply as soon as we're back.

Am I talking to a bot?

No. Every reply in this chat comes from a person on the team. We build AI assistants for clients, but we don't use one to answer you here.

Who owns the website and the automations?

You do. Domains, hosting accounts, code and automation workflows are set up in your business's name, and we document how everything works.

Is it safe to use AI with our customer data?

We set automations up so your team approves what goes out before it's sent. We use providers that don't train on your data and write down what runs where. Nothing changes without your sign-off.

Do you work with businesses outside our city?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses anywhere, by chat, video call and email.

What happens after launch?

Care plans cover hosting, updates, monitoring and small changes each month, with no long-term contract. Larger changes are quoted before we start.
